# Grants — conflicts, gaps and open questions

Compiled from the documents the colonel shared:

- `CEA 2026.pdf`
- `EDUCATION GRANT DIAV.pdf`
- `COMPUTER GRANT DIAV.pdf`
- `MARRIAGE GRANT DIAV.pdf`
- The green poster "Indian Army — Supporting Our Heroes Beyond the Battlefield"
- The green poster "Children Education Allowance (CEA)"
- The "Project Sambandh" posters
- `LIST OF FIELDS.xlsx` (both sheets)

Each item is marked:

- **CONFLICT**: two documents say different things
- **UNCLEAR**: a document leaves it open
- **MISSING DATA**: the form needs it, but we don't hold it

Until the colonel answers, the system uses the assumption written next to each item.

## 1. Education Grant (DIAV)

**What the documents agree on:** a yearly grant. Class 1–8 ₹10,000, Class 9–12 ₹14,000,
Graduation ₹20,000, Post Graduation ₹25,000, Professional course ₹50,000. Apply by
**30 November** each year. There is **no limit on the number of children**.

| # | Type | Issue |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | **CONFLICT** | **Who qualifies by cause of death.** The PDF says "spouse and wards of *physical casualties*", and the form says "physical/battle casualty". The poster says "including *suicide cases*", and its widow section says "widows of soldiers who *die in harness*" (any death in service). **Assumption for now: every death in service qualifies.** |
| E2 | **CONFLICT** | **Professional course amount.** The PDF says ₹50,000. The poster says "₹50,000 (Maximum)". Is it a fixed amount or a ceiling? |
| E3 | **UNCLEAR** | **Vocational courses.** The form lists "Vocational" as a level of education, but no amount is given for it anywhere. |
| E4 | **CONFLICT** | **What a widow can claim for.** The PDF says spouse eligible "Class I to Post Graduation". The poster lists widows only for Graduation, PG and Professional. |
| E5 | **UNCLEAR** | **"Professional course".** Which courses count (B.Tech, MBBS, nursing, ITI...)? This changes the amount from ₹20,000 to ₹50,000. |
| E6 | **MISSING DATA** | Class passed, academic year, university/board, percentage/CGPA, method of instruction (regular/correspondence), level of education, PAN (graduation and above), school tuition fee, **PPO number**, child's relationship to the deceased, house no, mohalla, post office, pension-account bank details, passport photos of the pensioner and the child. |

**Documents required:** application countersigned by the principal, original fee receipts
and mark sheet as on 31 March, copy of service booklet, copy of PPO, cancelled cheque or
passbook of the pension account.

## 2. Computer Grant (DIAV)

**What the documents agree on:** a **one-time ₹35,000**, for those who have **passed 10th
standard**. Apply by **30 November**. Same application form as the Education Grant
(strike out the one not applied for), but a separate form is needed per grant.

| # | Type | Issue |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | **UNCLEAR** | **One time per child, or per family?** |
| C2 | **CONFLICT** | **Invoice or quotation?** Page 1 says "invoice/bill of computer *purchased*". The form's note says "bill/invoice/*quotation*". Must the family buy the computer first, or can they apply with a quotation? |
| C3 | **UNCLEAR** | **Who qualifies by cause of death.** It says "physical casualties", the same question as E1. |
| C4 | **UNCLEAR** | "Education Entitlement Card (BC cases only)". Is BC = battle casualty? Where does this card come from? |
| C5 | **MISSING DATA** | 10th pass status or mark sheet, computer invoice, plus everything listed in E6 (it is the same form). |

## 3. Marriage Grant (DIAV)

**What the documents agree on:** a **one-time ₹1,00,000** for the remarriage of the widow,
the marriage of a daughter, or the marriage of an orphan son. The application must reach
DIAV **within one year of the marriage**.

| # | Type | Issue |
|---|---|---|
| M1 | **UNCLEAR** | **Who qualifies by cause of death.** Unlike the others, neither the PDF nor the poster says "physical casualty". Does every death qualify? |
| M2 | **UNCLEAR** | **How many times?** "One time" per daughter, or once per family? (Our old mock scheme assumed "maximum two daughters"; nothing in the real documents says so.) |
| M3 | **UNCLEAR** | **"Orphan" son.** It needs the mother's death certificate, so both parents must have died. We do not record whether the mother is alive. |
| M4 | **UNCLEAR** | "Name of operation (for BC cases only)". Only needed for battle casualties? |
| M5 | **UNCLEAR** | The form needs a recommendation from the CO/ZSWO/ST HQ. Is that done outside this system, or should we track it? |
| M6 | **MISSING DATA** | Bride's name and DOB, groom's name and DOB, date of marriage, place of marriage, marriage registering authority, date of registration, marriage certificate, PPO, pension-account bank details, passport photos. |

**Worth noting:** the "within one year of marriage" deadline could become an automatic reminder.

## 4. Children Education Allowance (CEA)

**What the documents agree on:** from the Government of India (not DIAV), for the **two
eldest children**, **KG to Class 12**, from academic year 2017-18. Double the amount for a
disabled child. Claimed **after the academic session ends**. Unclaimed years since 2017 can
be claimed as arrears. **It can be claimed alongside the DIAV Education Grant**; the PDF says
the DIAV grant "will continue as earlier".

| # | Type | Issue |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | **CONFLICT** | **Fees amount.** The PDF says ₹2,813/month. The CEA poster says ₹2,812/month. |
| A2 | **CONFLICT** | **When the new rate starts.** The PDF says "w.e.f. 01 Jan 2024". The poster says "2023-24 onwards" (an academic year that starts in 2023). |
| A3 | **CONFLICT** | **Age limit.** The poster says "up to 20 years". The PDF gives no age limit. |
| A4 | **CONFLICT** | **Name.** Every document says "Children Education Allowance". The Project Sambandh poster says "*Compassionate* Education Allowance". Same thing? |
| A5 | **UNCLEAR** | **Who qualifies by cause of death.** It says "death in harness, including physical casualty, missing in action, medical boarded out". This suggests a wide definition, the same question as E1. |
| A6 | **UNCLEAR** | **Where the claim goes depends on rank.** Officers → DIAV → CDA(O) Pune. JCO/OR → their Record Office. **We don't record which Record Office** a soldier belongs to. |
| A7 | **MISSING DATA** | Child's class, academic year, school name and place, school affiliation code and board, admission number, roll number, section, period claimed, amount claimed per child, correspondence address, hostel or not. |

**Rates as per the PDF (per month):** CEA ₹2,250 → ₹2,813 · Hostel ₹6,750 → ₹8,438 ·
CEA for a disabled child ₹4,500 → ₹5,625.

**Documents required:** contingent bill, self-declaration by the NOK, certificate from the
head of institution, cancelled cheque.
